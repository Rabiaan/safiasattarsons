02 How We Work

Four checkpoints between the field and your warehouse.

Every consignment follows the same disciplined path, which is why our delivery windows hold.

01

Sourcing

Direct contracts with select growers in Punjab, Sindh and the wider Indus basin, plus vetted international partners for oils and produce.

02

Milling & Sorting

Bühler ultra-clean milling, optical sorting and double polishing, with moisture and grain-length verification on every lot.

03

Quality Control

ISO 22000, HACCP and SASO conformity documentation issued with every consignment before it leaves the warehouse.

04

Freight & Clearance

Container bookings through Jeddah Islamic Port and Jebel Ali, with customs clearance and multi-warehouse distribution handled in-house.

03 Trade Desk

Questions we answer every day.

What are your minimum order quantities? add

We supply from single pallets for private-label packers up to full container loads of 25–27 MT for importers and hypermarket chains. Mixed container loading is available for selected rice and oil grades.

Which certifications do you ship with? add

Every consignment is accompanied by ISO 22000, HACCP and SASO conformity documentation, phytosanitary certificates where applicable, and third-party weight and quality inspection reports.

How quickly can you deliver to Gulf ports? add

Standard shipments clear Jeddah Islamic Port and Jebel Ali within seven to ten days of confirmed booking, with documentation prepared in parallel to freight execution.

Do you provide private-label packaging? add

Yes. We offer consumer bottles and pouches for oils, woven bags for rice at 5 kg, 10 kg, 20 kg and 40 kg, and flexitanks for bulk edible oil.
